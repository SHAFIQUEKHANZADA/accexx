6. Related Training & Delivery Terms
Terms used throughout the facilitator guides and textbooks that aren't program codes but come up often enough to belong in this glossary.
Term
What It Means
CEU
Continuing Education Unit — the standard unit for measuring non-credit continuing education, used throughout the library at a ratio of 1 CEU: 10 contact hours.
IACET
International Accreditors for Continuing Education and Training — the accrediting body that would need to formally register Accexx Insight's programs before recommended CEUs become officially issued credit, rather than a calculated estimate.
EQ
Emotional Intelligence (sometimes "Emotional Quotient") — a recurring skill area across the HOC Short Courses, Leadership Development, and Consulting libraries.
STAR
Situation, Task, Action, Result — the structured answer format taught in BEI-08 (Shoot to Score) for interview preparation.
ATS
Applicant Tracking System — the software many employers use to screen resumes, covered in BEI-06 (The Inside Track) and HR-05 (Recruitment & Selection for Culture Fit).
Two program families — Project Unify Certificate Programs (15 certificates) and HOC Short Courses & Workshops (8 courses) — don't yet have short-code systems like LD-, BEI-, HR-, or CS-, since they're still at the original curriculum-outline standard rather than the rebuilt facilitator-guide standard. See the Curriculum Library Master Index for their full names and current status.
