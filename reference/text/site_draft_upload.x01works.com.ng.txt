Meet Dr. A
Dr. Laide R.
Alexander.
Dr. Laide R. Alexander is a member of the Forbes Coaches Council, Founder & CEO of Accexx Insight LLC, and a distinguished leadership development practitioner. She has served as a college president, professor, and business founder. She currently serves as Regional Director of Enrollment for the State of Texas at Galen College of Nursing, where she leads growth, fiscal sustainability, and business continuity strategy. She is the author of The Unfinished Leader and Why Move My Cheese?, and host of the annual Why Move My Cheese? Conference.
