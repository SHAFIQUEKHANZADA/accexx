Executive Summary
Pricing is built bottom-up from each program's already-documented contact hours (for courses) or engagement phases (for consulting and coaching), using a tiered hourly rate, a standard cohort size assumption, and consistent delivery-format and package discounts. Every figure in this document can be traced back to the rate card on the next page.

Pricing Methodology & Assumptions
1. Courses (Flagship, Leadership Development, BEInspire, Project Unify, HOC Short Courses)
Priced per cohort (one delivered program, any group size up to the standard cap), calculated as:
Base Price = Tier Hourly Rate  ×  Program's Total Contact Hours
This is a per-cohort fee, not a per-participant fee — one client books the whole program for their group, up to the standard cohort cap listed below. Groups larger than the cap pay a per-additional-participant fee.

Delivery Format Adjustment
Format | Price | Rationale
In-Person | 100% of base price | Full rate — includes facilitator travel and venue-related overhead
Hybrid | 92.5% of base price | Partial reduction — some sessions still require in-person logistics
Virtual | 85% of base price | Reduced rate — no travel or venue costs
Cohort Size & Additional Participants
Standard cohort cap: 15 participants (20 for BEInspire, reflecting its classroom-style format).
Additional participant fee: 8% of the in-person base price per participant beyond the cap, up to a maximum cohort of 25.
2. Consulting
Priced by consulting day, using each engagement's already-documented phase timing to estimate the number of days required:
Engagement Fee = $2,800/day  ×  Estimated Consulting Days (derived from the engagement's phase timing)
For single-day engagements, this is a flat fee. For multi-week engagements, it's presented as a range, since actual scope varies by organization size — Finance should treat the low end as a lean engagement and the high end as a fuller one.
