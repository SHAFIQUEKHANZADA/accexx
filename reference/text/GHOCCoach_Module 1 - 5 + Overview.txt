2. Activity — Cross-Cultural Coaching Reflection Worksheet
Instructions: Reflect honestly on your own cultural lens as a coach, and how it might shape what you notice or miss with a client from a different background.
A real or anticipated coaching situation with a client from a cultural background different from my own:

G-HOC Coach Self-Paced — Module 5 Complete Package
Supervision & Case Work Practicum (Capstone)
Important Note Before This Module
This module is different from the previous four. Group supervision and supervised coaching sessions are inherently live, relational, and require a real supervisor and real peers — no self-paced content can substitute for them, and this module doesn't attempt to. What follows is an honest primer: what group supervision actually offers, and how to prepare a case study so it's genuinely ready to bring to real supervision. Completing this self-paced module prepares you for the live practicum — it does not replace it, and does not on its own satisfy G-HOC Coach's certification requirement.
1. Module Overview
Description
Learners prepare a real case study for group supervision, and understand what genuine supervised case work actually involves, ahead of completing the live practicum required for full certification.
Objective(s)
• Understand what group supervision offers that solo reflection cannot
• Prepare a case study ready for real supervision
