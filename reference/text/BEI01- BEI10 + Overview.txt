BEI-01 Self-Paced — Complete Package
Being a 10
This is a distinct, self-paced companion track — not the live BEI-01 workshop, and not a replacement for it. The live version is 3 contact hours, delivered in-person or live-virtual with real peer sharing and group energy that self-paced content can't replicate. This version is a short, individual primer covering the same four segments' core content.

Description
Raise your standards and set clear, compelling goals for education, career, and life. Build a real 90-day action plan you'll actually use.
Who This Is For

2. Activity — The Full Being a 10 Workbook
Instructions: Work through all four sections in order. Be honest, not impressive — this only works if your answers are real.
Section 1: Your Starting Point
On a scale of 1–10, how would you honestly rate where you are right now — in school, your career, or your life direction? No judgment, just honesty.
Section 2: Raising Your Standards
Where have you been quietly accepting less than you actually want, in one specific area?
What would a higher personal standard actually sound like, stated in your own words?
Section 3: Setting Compelling Goals
Write one goal in education, one in career, and one in life — each specific enough that you'd know exactly when you hit it.
Section 4: Your 90-Day Action Plan
Pick one goal from above. What will you actually do in the next 90 days to move it forward?
What's the one specific first step you'll take this week?

Submit your completed workbook, along with a short written statement (100–150 words) naming your first step and when, exactly, you'll take it.
