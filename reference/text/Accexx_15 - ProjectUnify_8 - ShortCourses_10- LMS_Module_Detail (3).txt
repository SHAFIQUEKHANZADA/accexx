Certificate in School to Work Readiness
Prepares final-year students and new graduates for the transition from school to the workplace — covering expectations, professional conduct, and practical job-search skills. (10 contact hours, 1 recommended CEU.)
Target Audience: Final-year students, interns, NYSC members, new graduates.
Prerequisites: None — open to any final-year student, intern, or recent graduate preparing to enter the workforce.
Certification Capstone
Workplace-Readiness Plan
A one-to-two page plan naming the participant's target role or field, three specific workplace behaviors they will practice in their first 90 days, and one concrete next step in their job search, submitted with a completed mock-interview reflection.
Module 1: What the Workplace Actually Expects
Duration: 3 contact hours
Expected Outcomes (Learning Objectives)
Distinguish school norms from workplace norms
Identify 3–5 unwritten expectations common across employers
Activities — In-Person Delivery
Activity | Time | What Happens
Norms Contrast Exercise | 30 min | Participants list school norms they rely on, then predict which will and won't transfer to a workplace.
Unwritten Rules Panel Discussion | 40 min | Facilitator (or invited early-career guest) walks through real unwritten expectations by industry.
Small-Group Debrief | 20 min | Groups identify which expectation feels least intuitive to them personally and why.
Activities — Online Delivery
Activity | Time | What Happens
Pre-Work Norms Survey | 15 min (async) | Participants complete a short self-assessment before the live session.
Live Unwritten Rules Discussion | 40 min | Facilitator-led discussion in the main room, using survey results to surface gaps.
Breakout Debrief | 20 min | Same debrief exercise conducted in breakout rooms.
Assessment / Check for Understanding
Each participant names one unwritten workplace expectation they had not previously considered and explains why it matters.
Materials & Resources
Norms contrast worksheet
Unwritten rules reference handout

Etiquette Scenario Cards | 30 min | Small groups work through common etiquette dilemmas (e.g., unclear dress code, being new in a meeting) and propose responses.
Activities — Online Delivery
Activity | Time | What Happens
Email Rewrite (Shared Doc) | 30 min | Participants complete the rewrite exercise in a shared document with live facilitator comments.
Video Role-Play | 40 min | Pairs conduct the supervisor role-play over video breakout, then debrief live.

Assessment / Check for Understanding

Materials & Resources

Certificate in Early Career Professionalism
Equips new hires and early-career professionals with the professionalism, communication, and self-management skills to succeed from Day 1. (10 contact hours, 1 recommended CEU.)
Target Audience: Interns, NYSC members, graduate trainees, staff in their first 3 years of work.
Prerequisites: None — designed for anyone in their first three years of employment.
Certification Capstone
Day 1–90 Action Plan
A structured plan naming what the participant will do in their first week, first 30 days, and first 90 days to establish professionalism, communication habits, and feedback-seeking behavior, with at least one measurable indicator per phase.
Module 1: Professionalism From Day 1

Expected Outcomes (Learning Objectives)
Identify the specific behaviors that signal professionalism in a new role
Recognize how first impressions compound over the first 90 days
Activities — In-Person Delivery
Activity | Time | What Happens

Contrast Case Discussion | 35 min | Facilitator presents the two new-hire stories; groups identify the specific decision points that diverged.
Self-Assessment | 25 min | Participants privately rate their own current habits against the identified behaviors.
Activities — Online Delivery
Activity | Time | What Happens
Pre-Work Reflection Prompt | 10 min (async) | Participants submit one past experience of a strong or weak first impression before the session.
Live Contrast Case Discussion | 35 min | Same discussion, using submitted pre-work as additional examples.
Self-Assessment (Private Poll) | 20 min | Self-assessment conducted via anonymous live poll, with aggregate results discussed.
Assessment / Check for Understanding
Each participant names one behavior from their self-assessment they will actively work on and why.
Materials & Resources
First-impressions worksheet
Self-assessment checklist

Supports supervisors, team leads, and young managers in shifting from individual contributor to leader mindset — with EQ tools and a practical 60–90 day plan. (6 contact hours.)
Target Audience: Supervisors, team leads, heads of department, young managers, high-potential staff.
Prerequisites: None — most relevant for those newly or soon stepping into a leadership role.
Assessment & Completion
Completion is based on attendance and participation in all modules, plus a completed 60–90 Day Self-Leadership Plan. Participants receive a Certificate of Participation.
60–90 Day Self-Leadership Plan
A short plan naming the participant's top three leadership responsibilities, one low-value activity they will stop or delegate, and one EQ tool they will use consistently over the next 60–90 days.
Module 1: Individual Contributor vs. Leader Mindset
Duration: 2 contact hours
Expected Outcomes (Learning Objectives)
Identify the core mindset shift required in a leadership role
Recognize personal habits still anchored in an individual-contributor mindset
Activities — In-Person Delivery
Activity | Time | What Happens

Case Study Analysis | 35 min | Groups analyze the case and identify the specific moment the shift should have happened.
Self-Identification | 20 min | Participants privately identify one habit still anchored in IC mindset.
Activities — Online Delivery
Activity | Time | What Happens
Mindset Contrast Discussion | 30 min | Same discussion delivered live.
Case Study Breakouts | 35 min | Analysis conducted in breakout groups.
Self-Identification (Private) | 20 min | Identification completed individually and privately.
Assessment / Check for Understanding
Each participant names one specific IC habit they need to shift.
Materials & Resources
